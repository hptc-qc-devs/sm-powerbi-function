Before you start

You need a SurveyMonkey app to get an access token. It takes a minute and you only do it once.

  1. Go to developer.surveymonkey.com/apps and sign in.
  2. Create a private app. Private apps need no review and work immediately.
  3. Under Scopes, enable View Surveys and View Responses. Those are the only two needed — this tool never writes to your SurveyMonkey account.
  4. Open the app's Settings tab and copy its Access Token.

Connect SurveyMonkey

The simplest route. Paste the access token from your app's Settings tab. It is checked against SurveyMonkey before being saved, so a typo is caught here rather than showing up later as a failed sync.
